## 管 AI 五件事

五个可以被检视的管理动作。不是五个能力，是五件可以开会检查的事。

| # | 动作 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 给 AI 派活 | 哪些活交给 AI，哪些留给人。分不清这条线，用多少工具都不算数 |
| 2 | 给 AI 定标准 | 交付判据要写在动手之前。说不清什么叫做好，就没有办法验收 |
| 3 | 验真伪 | 看得出产出的哪一段是编的。这是管理者最难替代的一项判断 |
| 4 | 进考核 | AI 用得怎么样，要进绩效面谈。不进考核的动作，三个月就会消失 |
| 5 | 带团队 | 下属的 AI 能力要分层评估，再分层训练。不能假设大家在同一水平线上 |

## 这是一个可以到达的状态

AI 管理者不是先天的出身，是可以通过学习、实践、工具与服务达成的状态。这句话是整个方法体系的前提：如果它不成立，诊断、培训、陪跑都没有意义。

旧的管理者不需要被替换，需要的是把 AI 纳入自己的管理动作。

## English

**The Manager Who Harnesses AI · Senior Vice President, Strategy Development and Investment · Chief Strategy Officer, AI Office, CGL Group**

> An **AI Manager** is a manager who has brought AI into everyday managerial actions — judged not by tool proficiency, but by whether the way they assign work, review results and lead people has changed because of AI.

I run the AI Office at CGL Group and teach other managers how to harness AI in theirs. These are the same job.

**Why "harness" and not "manage".** Managing AI reads two ways: running an AI system, or running an AI department. Both send the reader to the wrong place. Harnessing has one meaning — a person puts AI to work and answers for what comes out.

I wrote the definition of the AI Manager in November 2026. No one had defined the term before that.

**Five Things to Manage AI**

1. Assign work to AI — decide what goes to AI and what stays with people
2. Set the standard first — define acceptance criteria before the work starts
3. Verify — tell which part of an output is fabricated
4. Put it into performance review — what is not reviewed disappears in three months
5. Lead the team — assess and train people at different AI levels separately

**This is a reachable state, not a birthright.** Managers trained before AI can become AI Managers through learning, practice, tools and services.
